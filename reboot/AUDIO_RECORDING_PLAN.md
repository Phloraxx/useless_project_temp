# Authentic Audio Recording Plan

The CC0 library is for prototyping. Signature vehicle/interior/voice audio should be recorded locally with permission.

## Bus session capture list

Record isolated or as cleanly as possible:
- exterior idle front/rear/side;
- interior idle at driver and mid-cabin positions;
- gentle, medium and high-load acceleration runs;
- constant-speed interior/exterior passes;
- throttle lift/coast;
- brake application + air release/hiss variants;
- door open/close cycles;
- short/medium/held horn variants from exterior and interior;
- conductor bell/button/buzzer if available;
- indicator relay/dashboard switches;
- wiper low/high and blade contact;
- suspension/body rattles over rough road/speed breaker;
- rain on roof/window if an actual rainy session is possible.

Capture room tone before/after every setup.
## Kerala ambience sessions

Capture several 2–5 minute stereo beds plus isolated foreground events from places where recording is lawful and unobtrusive:
- ordinary junction with autos/bikes/buses;
- quieter residential roadside;
- tea-shop/commercial frontage with permission;
- bus-stop crowd texture without identifiable private conversation as a hero asset;
- open paddy/green roadside;
- monsoon rain under roof/awning;
- wet-road vehicle passes.

Avoid publishing private conversations as character dialogue. Ambient speech should either be intentionally performed/consented or sufficiently non-identifiable background texture.

## Voice session

Record examiner/conductor/passenger actors separately in a controlled room when possible. Preserve 48 kHz/24-bit WAV masters. Slate character, line ID, take and region/register pool.

For every hero line, record: neutral natural take, slightly faster conversational take, and one actor-led alternative wording. The actor-led take is often the preferred final line if it preserves intent.
