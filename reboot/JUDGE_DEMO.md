# അടുത്ത സ്റ്റോപ്പിൽ™ — Judge Demo Route

## 2–3 minute showcase

1. Open the default build and enter any candidate name. Press **BEGIN QUALIFICATION**. The click also attempts to start the bus audio layer.
2. At the first bus stop (~102 m), intentionally stop 2–4 m away from the exact marker. Show the **Passenger Fitness** increase and examiner camera reaction.
3. Use one short `H` horn and pass the auto near ~218 m with clear commitment. Do not hit it: real obstacle contact can end the exam.
4. Carry reasonable speed through the chicane and speed breaker. Point out **Decorative Line Recognition** and **Suspension Trust Exercise**.
5. The wet-road section flips the joke: the same aggressive inputs now attract examiner concern because the road no longer supports them.
6. Maintain momentum through the rough section, then negotiate the two-vehicle **Queue Allergy** gap around 550–590 m.
7. At the second stop (~625 m), stop a few metres off the exact point again or deliberately convert it into a fictional walking programme.
8. From ~675–825 m, build speed on the dry final stretch. One horn during the rival-service section improves **Timetable Diplomacy**.
9. Reach the depot near 900 m. The bus is frozen and a personalised fictional qualification certificate is generated from the run metrics.

## Failure demo (optional)

Retry and hit the auto/barrier at meaningful speed. Rapier collision telemetry forces **DISQUALIFIED — ACTUAL VEHICLE CONTACT**, demonstrating that the game rewards reckless-looking control, not actual crashing.
