# Malayalam movie dialogue cameo rules

The game should **not** use a famous line simply because the player succeeded. A quote is funny only when the gameplay situation recreates the intention that made the original scene memorable.

No movie audio/video is bundled. These are short, attributed, text-only candidates.

| Quote | Film | Original scene intention | Correct game analogue | Wrong use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `സവാരി ഗിരിഗിരി` | *Ravanaprabhu* (2001) | swagger, one-upmanship, putting somebody in their place | win a specific rivalry/showdown against a recurring rival bus/driver | ordinary overtake, normal fast driving |
| `നീ പോ മോനേ ദിനേശാ` | *Narasimham* (2000) | dismissive dominance aimed at a specific person, Dineshan | dismiss an established named nuisance character after a scene has earned it | cones, autos, random road users |
| `ഓർമ്മയുണ്ടോ ഈ മുഖം?` | *Commissioner* (1994) | confrontational recognition between characters with history | a recurring rival/person reappears and recognizes the player | restart/retry screen |
| `അങ്ങനെ പവനായി ശവമായി!` | *Nadodikkaattu* (1987) | the much-hyped Pavanayi/plan ends in spectacular failure | an elaborate non-injury scheme/prop collapses after being overhyped | crash, passenger injury, ordinary mistake |
| `പോളണ്ടിനെപ്പറ്റി നീ ഒരക്ഷരം മിണ്ടരുത്.` | *Sandesam* (1991) | stopping an inconvenient political counterargument in an ideological quarrel | NPC argument spirals into irrelevant theory and one person bans the topic | random examiner reaction |

## Runtime policy

1. Establish the people/situation first.
2. Let the player understand the conflict without the quote.
3. Use the quote only if its original intention maps cleanly onto the current beat.
4. Never explain the reference in the dialogue itself. Attribution can live in an optional UI/subtitle metadata layer.
5. A normal run should contain very few movie cameos; original spoken Malayalam remains the main voice of the game.
6. Never use a comic movie quote after a real injury, pedestrian impact, or serious collision.
