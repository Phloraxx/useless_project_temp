# അടുത്ത സ്റ്റോപ്പിൽ™ — Reboot Product Truth

Status: **ACTIVE REBOOT TRUTH — 2026-09-11**

The currently deployed prototype is a preserved reference, not the target product. Reboot work stays under `reboot/` until a new vertical slice clearly beats it.

## Product fantasy

This is no longer five separate tests. The player takes one absurdly serious, potentially endless Kerala-bus driving aptitude test.

A driving examiner sits inside the bus with a clipboard. A conductor and a changing population of passengers ride with the player. The road keeps unfolding through a stylized, socially dense Kerala. The examiner grades events in real time.

The central inversion remains: ordinary textbook driving can be marked as evidence that the candidate lacks the fictional "natural bus-driver instinct". The game may reward controlled assertiveness, comic stop placement, horn communication, confident ETA answers and maintaining flow.

Reckless-looking authored driving can be rewarded as part of the parody, especially on dry/clear roads. Serious collisions, pedestrian impacts and loss of control are still major penalties or disqualification; consequence is the boundary.

## Core loop

`drive → encounter → player decision → world reaction → examiner judgement → passenger reaction → score/combo → continue`

There are no level-complete screens during normal play. Encounters occur organically on one continuous route.

## Run structure

- Begin at a fictional depot/stand with a short real-time introduction.
- Stream an indefinite route from hand-authored Kerala road chunks.
- Escalate encounter density and combinations rather than numbered levels.
- Build `Natural Aptitude`, `Flow`, distance and examiner approval.
- "Too textbook" behaviour triggers a short cinematic grading interruption.
- End only on disqualification, player quit, or an intentionally chosen run end.
## Tone and story

This is a character comedy, not a KSRTC insult simulator. The ridiculous institution/examiner owns the absurd standards. Passengers may disagree, panic, laugh, complain or simply ignore the examiner. The conductor has separate priorities.

The story is the run: the candidate slowly learns what this examiner considers "talent" while the social world keeps reacting.

## Visual target

Not flat low-poly boxes. Not photorealism. Not an asset-pack diorama.

Target: **Bully-era authored density + Kerala roadside specificity + modern stylized WebGL rendering.**

The bus must have a believable partial interior. Shops must look inhabited. People need routines and animation. Lighting/materials must create depth even when nothing funny is happening.

## Language truth

Final spoken Malayalam is written for the mouth first and subtitles second.

- Formal Malayalam belongs mainly to deliberately bureaucratic UI/clipboard text.
- Spoken dialogue may contract words, omit subjects, code-switch and use regional rhythm.
- District accents are reviewed/cast with actual speakers rather than generated from stereotypes.
- Subtitles preserve the spoken line instead of correcting it to print Malayalam.

## Driving truth

Driving must be fun with all dialogue muted. The player must feel bus mass, suspension, steering, braking, road surface and speed through physics + camera + animation + audio.

The new controller will be a dynamic raycast-wheel vehicle. The old forward-vector kinematic controller is retired for reboot work.
## World / character / audio truth

"Infinite" means recombining authored road modules, not procedural mush. Chunks own road spline, shoulder/drain, social spaces, spawn sockets, audio zones, encounter sockets and camera anchors.

Examiner, conductor and passengers physically exist. They idle, look, talk, board, sit, stand, brace, sway and react to vehicle motion. Animation is gameplay feedback, not decoration.

No oscillator horn and no one-loop engine. Final sound uses layered diesel/load, drivetrain/body rattle, air brake, road/tire, horn variants, bell, door, wipers, weather, traffic, shops/crowds and positional human dialogue.

Cinematics are short, real-time and integrated. Freeze/slow only in deterministic safe states, cut to examiner/passenger/road detail, land one line, then return to chase view.

## Reboot non-goals

- multiplayer/network sync now;
- remote leaderboard/backend now;
- open-world walking/free roaming;
- licensed real KSRTC branding or exact route replication;
- giant map;
- full bus-management economy;
- dozens of vehicles before the hero bus feels good;
- generative AI dialogue at runtime;
- speech recognition dependency.

Local high scores are enough initially. Multiplayer/global leaderboards remain future design questions.

## Success test

The reboot only succeeds if someone can drive for five minutes with no explanation and say: **the bus feels good, the road feels Kerala, the people feel alive, the examiner is funny, and I want another run for a higher score.**
