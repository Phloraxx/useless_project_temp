## Important provenance notes

- `diesel-pullaway-831721.mp3` is verified CC0 and retained as a **reference/calibration recording**, not a runtime layer, because it already contains its own acceleration and multiple gear changes; playing it continuously would fight telemetry-driven gear state. Source: https://freesound.org/s/831721/ .
- `horn-india-bus-508540.mp3` is verified CC0 and retained as an alternate/world-horn candidate, not the player horn runtime source. Source: https://freesound.org/s/508540/ .
- `interior-rattle-386401.mp3` is verified CC0 but rejected from runtime because it is a tool-case rattle rather than a vehicle recording. Source: https://freesound.org/s/386401/ .
- `road-asphalt-interior-685074.mp3` is retained as a researched candidate but not used in runtime; `860717` is the currently documented road/cabin prototype.
- Current engine set is prototype engineering material. The Audio Bible still calls for authentic local heavy Kerala-bus recordings for signature engine, horn, interior, doors and rattles.
- Kerala ambience contains naturally occurring background speech, not scripted hero dialogue. Do not repurpose it as character dialogue.
